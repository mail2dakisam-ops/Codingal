Introduction

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam (1931–2015) was a distinguished Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. Widely revered as the "Missile Man of India," he played a foundational role in the nation's civilian space program and military missile development.

Words of Wisdom

"To succeed in your mission, you must have single-minded devotion to your goal."

Early Life and Education

Born into a humble family in Rameswaram, Dr. Kalam learned the value of hard work early in life.

  1. Early Struggles: Worked as a newspaper boy to support his family.
  2. Academic Excellence: Earned a degree in Physics from St. Joseph's College.
  3. Specialized Engineering: Graduated in Aerospace Engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology.

Scientific Legacy

ISRO & Space Exploration
Played a critical role in the development of India's space program and was the project director for the SLV-III.
Defense & Missiles
Spearheaded the Integrated Guided Missile Development Program, resulting in missiles like Agni and Prithvi.

The People's President

Serving as President from 2002 to 2007, he became known as the "People's President."

Legacy: Even after his passing in 2015, his books like Wings of Fire continue to inspire millions. You can learn more about his life at the official Kalam Centre website.
