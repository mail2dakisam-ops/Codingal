INTRO:


Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1931–2015) was an eminent aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. Known as the "Missile Man of India", he played a pivotal role in the nation's civilian space program and military missile development. Post-presidency, he dedicated his life to teaching and mentoring India's youth.

Born into a humble family—his father was a boat owner and imam—Dr. Kalam learned the value of hard work early on, even selling newspapers to supplement his family's income. He developed a deep fascination with flight at a young age. He graduated in physics from St. Joseph's College in 1954 and went on to study aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology in Chennai.
