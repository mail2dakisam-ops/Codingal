Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

INTRO

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and statesman. He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was born and raised in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, and studied physics and aerospace engineering.

"Dream is not that which you see while sleeping it is something that does not let you sleep."

National Award: He received India's highest civilian honor, the Bharat Ratna.
Legacy: He is fondly remembered as the People's President.

Kalam initially wanted to become a fighter pilot scientist. His scientific work advanced chemical compounds like H2O and rocketry dynamics. He launched India's 2nd nuclear test program at Pokhran in 1998.

One of his famous sayings is: Failure will never overtake me if my determination to succeed is strong enough. You can read more about him on his official Wikipedia Profile.

Born 15 October 1931
Died 27 July 2015
