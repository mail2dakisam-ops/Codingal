Discovering the wonders of our natural world

Forests

Forests are home to over 80% of the world's terrestrial biodiversity. They act as the planet's lungs, absorbing carbon dioxide and releasing vital oxygen.

Rivers

Rivers are freshwater lifelines that carve landscapes and support civilizations. They provide fresh water, transportation, and diverse aquatic habitats.

Mountains

Mountains cover about one-quarter of the Earth's land surface. They influence global climate patterns and provide fresh water to half of humanity.

Wildlife

Wildlife forms a complex web of life that maintains ecosystems balanced and healthy. Protecting diverse animal species ensures our planet's survival.
